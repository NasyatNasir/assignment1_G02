## 2. Roles

Coordinator (keeps the plan and the issue list), designer (docs/design.md and the
hand trace), implementer (src/a1_triage.py), tester (tests and ruff), reviewer (reads every change
before it is merged). Everybody writes code and everybody commits. Roles rotate after the first
two weeks.

The rotation of roles happened by shifting the members' role.
For example: Member 1 will take Member 2's roles, Member 2 will take Member 3's  Role, 
Member 3 will take Member 4's role, and Member 4 will take Member 1's role.

## 3. How we make decisions

In a time where decision need to be taken, everyone will discuss it together and produce a 
few options to be considered. Once the options are clearly defined, a poll on the telegram 
channel will be conducted. Decision will be taken depending on the highest number of vote. 
If there are options with same number of vote, coordinator at the moment will make a decision 
based on options with highest number of votes.
